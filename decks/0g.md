# 0G — Physical AI pitch (8 slides)

**Deck style:** black, monospace, one idea per slide. Numbers are from the board, not estimates.

---

**1 · Cover**
ASH · a seal that destroys the data if you open it
0G Physical AI · ETHGlobal Mumbai 2026

**2 · The body**
A pocket device with one job: hold the key to a sealed case.
The MCU owns the loop, the key, and the clock. The model lives off-device.

**3 · Where 0G sits**
Tier 1: an ambiguous electrical event → the MCU goes amber and sends a compact feature vector.
0G returns `hold | wipe` inside a 5 s deadline.
The model can hold until the deadline or ask for a wipe. It can never un-wipe.

**4 · What it costs, measured**
2.05 s median · p95 2.11 s · $0.000038 per decision · request id `3538c131…`
A sealed case makes roughly one such decision in its life.

**5 · Why this is 0G-native, not 0G-decorated**
Remove 0G and the seal falls back to "any interruption wipes" — safe and unusable.
The model does not annotate the decision; it *is* the decision, bounded by a deadline.
No fallback fakes a verdict: a timeout logs `0G_UNAVAILABLE` and wipes.

**6 · The failure that sells it**
Wi-Fi drops mid-handling. The device does not guess, and it does not wait forever.
It wipes at the deadline and records why. That is the sentence a risk officer buys.

iAI credits for the operator's own inference budget.
